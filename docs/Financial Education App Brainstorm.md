BUBU \- Learning App for Finance Literacy

**I. Features**

1. AI Chatbot (Dr.Bos):

- Serves as an assistant to learners and provides guidance needed.
- Uses flowcharts, diagrams, and visualizations to summarize or explain ideas.
- Refers to textbook information and asks guided questions (Socratic method) to help students build critical thinking.
- Doesn’t give advice on investment or important life decisions.
- Learns about the student through conversation to self-improve.

3. Student portfolio/dashboard

- Keeps track on student’s progress
- Analyzes student’s habits, strengths, and areas for growth
- Portfolio: financial wealth, wallet (digital coin used in the app), analytical charts

4. Story world

- Character Bubu—mascot of the app and student’s learning buddy.
- Bubu will appear across decision-making activities
- Solve real case scenarios in Bubu’s world, such as managing personal expenses, saving, budgeting, solving unexpected scenarios (get scammed, sick,...), opening a credit card/bank account, etc. (Bubu’s world is a simulation of real world situations, where Bubu is a person like us).

5. Project workspace

- Students come up with ideas or tackle provided projects:

* Student’s project: fundraising, investing, even planning, seeking sponsorships,... This space serves as a Notion-like environment for students to brainstorm ideas and organize materials, coordinate tasks,... and get advice from Dr.Bos.
* Provided project: community-open space to discuss and collaboratively or individually solve the real cases (like Roblox or Minecraft).
