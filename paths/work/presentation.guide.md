# Present your project

Use this eight-slide template to tell the story of your team's game. Aim for a
short pitch with one live demonstration; adjust the timing to your competition.

[@link.play.slides Present the project](./project-presentation.deck.md)

[Read the deck](./project-presentation.deck.md)

## Make it yours

Edit `paths/work/project-presentation.deck.md`. Replace every square-bracket
prompt with your own words, including the project title and team names. Each
`---` separator starts a new slide. Keep each slide to one idea and a few short
lines. Add an actual screenshot if it helps explain your work.

The deck covers the problem, learner, learning goal, Learn/Try/Review loop, live
demo, team contributions, testing evidence, and next steps. Name what came from
the starter and what your team created. Report real observations; do not invent
user research, scores, or outcomes.

## Rehearse in PathMX

1. Use **Present the project** above to launch Slides. Use Player's navigation
   and its shortcut help to rehearse advancing and going back.
2. Open the game in a separate browser tab before starting. At the demo slide,
   switch to it, show one complete interaction, then return to your deck.
3. Pick who speaks on each slide. Practice once with a timer and once with the
   actual projector or shared screen.
4. Check that every placeholder is replaced and text fits. Keep a screenshot
   ready if your live demo depends on a service that may be unavailable.

The deck is ordinary PathMX Markdown, rendered by the installed Player plugin.
Use the reading view while editing and Slides while presenting. Keep the
`presentation.layout.md` import so the Player remains available.

## Ask your agent

```text
Read AGENTS.md and paths/work/project-presentation.deck.md.
Help us turn our actual work into a short presentation.
Ask us what we built, who we tested with, and what we observed.
Keep our answers accurate and our language simple. Do not invent evidence.
Leave placeholders where we have not provided information.
```
