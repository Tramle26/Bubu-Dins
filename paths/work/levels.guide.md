# Lesson library

`/learn.page` is the course list. The Learn nav link, home “Let’s explore”
button, and journey “View all” links open it directly. There is no middle
school / high school / college picker on that path.

Edit `paths/learn.page.md` to add or change a playable course card. When a
lesson is built, replace its Coming soon entry with a real Source link.
Follow the building guide to connect a journey to Completion. Do not create a
second progress store or award coins for opening Learn.

Optional topic lists still live at `paths/learn/middle.page.md` and
`high.page.md`. They are not linked from Learn.
