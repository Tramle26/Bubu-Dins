# Housing practice expectations

Dr.Bos evaluates a beginner's explanation of both fictional Sam cases. All four
criteria must be met to pass. A few clear sentences can satisfy them; do not require
calculations, technical vocabulary, perfect grammar, or a particular renting/buying choice.
Do not pass empty praise, copied instructions, unrelated text, or unexplained choices.

- **Realistic:** Uses plausible housing reasoning without a major misconception
  (for example, buying always makes money, or a down payment is simply a lost fee).
- **Possible:** Identifies a workable next thing to investigate, including at least
  one missing cost, number, or assumption. Exact prices or research are not required.
- **Applicable:** Addresses both Case A and Case B and connects the explanation to
  their different time horizons and cash needs. Brief, everyday language is enough.
- **Impactful:** Explains at least one meaningful consequence for Sam, such as
  emergency cash, moving flexibility, selling costs, or responsibility for repairs.
  The consequence must support the reasoning, not merely list a keyword.

Credit overlapping evidence across criteria. Judge the explanation's meaning, not
its length or polish. A modest, reasoned answer should pass; vague claims alone should not.
When a criterion is missing, explain the gap kindly and give one focused instructor
hint. Do not give the complete model answer. Allow unlimited revisions and retries.

## How it works

The server sends the answer, this rubric, and the housing lessons to the configured
Dr.Bos provider. It validates four criterion results and exact evidence quotes,
then saves the submitted answer and latest feedback in private learner State.
PathMX Completion derives this lesson's pass from that saved result. A prior pass
remains earned during later practice. Other lessons still use learner acknowledgment.
Old self-check completion on Try it does not count as an assessed pass.

Missing credentials, timeout, or invalid output never grant a pass. AI judgment can
be imperfect; this is beginner practice feedback, not a certified mastery assessment.
Retries are unlimited, with a short six-requests-per-minute pause to protect the service.
Closing or reloading restores the last evaluated answer; unsubmitted edits are temporary.
See [AI setup](./ai-setup.guide.md) to configure the server. Chat remains a separate tutor
and cannot pass this lesson. Review provider judgments with representative learner answers.
