# Build Bubu together

This is your team's workspace. Bubu gives you one working learning journey;
you bring the ideas, lessons, and gameplay. Start with a small change you can
show another teammate, then build on it.

## Your build path

1. [Start and explore](./work/start.guide.md) — run the game, try a lesson, and choose your first change.
2. [Build a playable lesson](./work/building.guide.md) — copy the housing pattern and connect real saved progress.
3. [Create art and sprites](./work/art.guide.md) — keep characters consistent and images usable in the game.
4. [Work with the characters](./work/characters.guide.md) — write Bubu's nudges and prototype Dr.Bos's tutoring voice.
5. [Build with your agent](./work/agents.guide.md) — give clear tasks, keep control, and understand the result.
6. [Check and demo](./work/demo.guide.md) — test your change and hand it to a teammate.
7. [Present your project](./work/presentation.guide.md) — customize the deck and rehearse in PathMX Player.

## Student project space

[Open Project workspace](/project-workspace.page) to collect ideas, materials, and tasks.

## Pick a useful next piece

[Build with Dr.Bos's live visual thread](./work/dr-boss-chat.guide) — the working
prototype, visual examples, and small next steps for your team.

- **Lessons:** deepen the housing examples or plan the investing Learn → Try it → Review sequence.
- **Gameplay:** turn one Storyworld room into a small choice with a clear consequence.
- **Design:** improve one screen at phone and laptop sizes, including keyboard use.
- **Characters:** write helpful hints or create one consistent, transparent sprite pose.

Agree who owns each piece before editing shared files. The home, map, and housing
lessons are examples to learn from; the rest of the course is yours to make.
