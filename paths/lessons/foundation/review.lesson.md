# Put your plan into words

**College · Review · 3–5 minutes**

Maya earns **$1,200 a month before tax**, has **$70 left after taxes and expenses**,
and has **$800 set aside for tuition due in eight weeks**. She wants to start
investing for retirement. A friend says a stock fund guarantees growth.

In your own words, write **3–5 sentences** explaining:

1. How much Maya could start saving, and why it fits her budget.
2. What to do with the tuition money, and why its deadline matters.
3. One fee or diversification check before investing, and one risk she should understand.
