## Part B: Explore Capital One Nessie data

The dashboard below imports **simulated** accounts, purchases, and loans through
the server-side Nessie integration. A fresh sandbox may contain no records; that
empty state is still useful for seeing which pieces a complete portfolio needs.

<x-bubu-nessie-portfolio />

Try each tab:

1. **Accounts:** identify checking, savings, and credit-card balances. Explain why
   the snapshot subtracts card balances.
2. **Purchases:** enter take-home money, inspect the 50/30/20 targets, and revise
   at least one category when the description alone is not enough.
3. **Loans:** choose a student loan or use the sample balance. Enter an illustrative
   APR, then compare the original monthly payment with an extra payment.

The payoff explorer uses monthly amortization. It assumes a fixed rate and on-time
payments, excludes fees and changing repayment plans, and never sends a payment.
