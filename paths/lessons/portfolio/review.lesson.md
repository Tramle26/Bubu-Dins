# Review

Bring the pieces together: check what you own and owe, cover essentials, then explain how you would use what remains. A useful plan states its assumptions and the information still missing.

## Plan Jordan’s month

Jordan takes home $1,240. Rent is $600, food $260, transit $40, and phone $35. Jordan has $415 in savings, a $180 card balance, and an $18,000 subsidized student loan.

1. How much is left after essentials?
2. How would you divide it between the card and savings?
3. What loan or payment detail would you check before deciding?

Try it on paper or explain your answer aloud, then compare below.
