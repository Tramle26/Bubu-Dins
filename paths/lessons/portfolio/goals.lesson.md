# Saving goals

The date you need your money helps you decide where to keep it. Money needed soon needs stability and access. Longer-term money may have time to recover from market losses, but returns are never guaranteed.

Compounding means earning returns on earlier returns. Time can help, but fees, losses, and withdrawals affect the result.

## Sort by time

Put these goals into under one year, one to five years, or more than five years: a flight in three months, applications in two years, a car in three years, and retirement in forty-five years.
