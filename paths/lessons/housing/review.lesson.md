# Explain your choice to Dr.Bos

> “What would change your mind?” — Dr.Bos

Dr.Bos asks you to support a decision, not guess a preferred answer.
For this starter, the review is an **authored self-check**. No AI tutor or automatic
grader is connected.

Choose one of Sam's cases. Explain:

1. Two costs beyond the loan payment, including when they are paid.
2. Why two people with the same monthly budget could reasonably choose different homes.
3. How time, available cash, and uncertainty affect your choice.

<details>
<summary>Review your explanation</summary>

Did you distinguish an upfront fee from equity? Did you connect each choice to the person’s
time horizon and available cash? Did you name something that could change your conclusion?
Both renting and buying can be reasonable conclusions with different assumptions.

</details>
