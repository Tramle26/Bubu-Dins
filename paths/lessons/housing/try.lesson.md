# Try a housing comparison

<bubu-coach>Change one assumption. Notice what changes in your decision.</bubu-coach>

Compare two situations, then explain your thinking to Dr.Bos.

**Case A:** Sam expects to move in two years and needs access to emergency cash.
**Case B:** Sam expects to stay for ten years and has cash set aside for repairs.

For each case, choose what you would investigate first: the flexibility of renting,
or the longer-term costs and responsibilities of buying. Explain why. There is no
automatic winning choice; the missing numbers and assumptions matter.

Dr.Bos checks whether your explanation is **realistic, possible, applicable, and
impactful**. Address both cases, name a cost or assumption to check, and explain a
consequence for Sam. A few clear sentences are enough; no calculations are needed.
Spelling and grammar are not graded.
