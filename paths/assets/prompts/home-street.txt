Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide reusable background plate for the Bubu homepage

Using the supplied street mockup for composition and the grove painting for palette
and rendering style, create a clean 16:9 town street. Pale stone pavement with a
zebra crosswalk in the foreground center. Cream bank with columns and a gold coin
emblem on the left. Small cafe with a coral-and-white striped awning on the right.
Blue bus-stop post at the far right. Trees, planters, and a pale blue sky.

Keep the center of the street and the lower-middle crosswalk open for an HTML
character sprite. Scenery only: no people, animals, mascots, buttons, cards,
navigation, interface, readable words, numbers, logos, or watermark.
